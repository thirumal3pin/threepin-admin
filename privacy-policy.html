Privacy Policy

Last updated:

3 PIN Realty ("we", "us") provides a WhatsApp-based assistant and CRM to help customers find real estate listings and follow up with leads. This policy explains what data we collect and how it's used.

What we collect

  • WhatsApp conversations: messages you send to our WhatsApp number, so our team (and an AI assistant) can respond and follow up.
  • Contact details: name, phone number, and anything you share about your property requirements (area, budget, configuration).
  • Lead-form submissions: if you submit a Facebook/Instagram lead ad, the details you provided in that form.

How we use it

To respond to your enquiry, match you with relevant listings, and have a team member follow up. Messages are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI to draft replies; data is stored using Google Firebase/Firestore.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It's shared only with the service providers above (Meta/WhatsApp for messaging, Anthropic for AI processing, Google Firebase for storage) strictly to provide this service.

How long we keep it

We keep lead and enquiry data for as long as we are actively working with you, and for up to 24 months after our last contact, so we can pick a conversation back up if you return to us. After that it is deleted from our CRM.

Lead-form submissions from Facebook and Instagram lead ads are retrieved from Meta shortly after you submit them and stored in our own CRM. Meta separately deletes its copy 90 days after submission, under Meta's own policy.

Where we are required to keep records for tax, legal, or dispute-resolution reasons, we retain only what that requires, for only as long as it requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will confirm your identity, action the request within 30 days, and tell you when it is done.

Deleting your data removes your contact details, your enquiry, and the notes and conversation history attached to it from our CRM. You can also withdraw consent to be contacted at any time without deleting your record.

If you submitted a Facebook or Instagram lead form and want that data removed, you can request deletion either by emailing us or through Meta, which forwards the request to us automatically at our data deletion endpoint. Either route reaches us.
