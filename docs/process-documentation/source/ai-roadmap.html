Flagship 1 · nobody ships this full chain yet

One sentence books the whole site visit

The agent speaks or types a command. The assistant finds the client and the property, picks a free agent, confirms with the client on WhatsApp, reminds the agent and adds a task.

“Schedule the site visit for client Shiva at Anne property, 2 pm tomorrow.”

TODAYBy hand

  1. 1Search and open Shiva's lead
  2. 2Open the site-visit scheduler
  3. 3Pick the day and time
  4. 4Check who is free on Google Calendar
  5. 5Choose the agent and save
  6. 6Find the property's map link
  7. 7WhatsApp Shiva the time, location and contact
  8. 8Remember to remind the agent

8 manual steps across 3 apps

WITH AIOne command, one tap

  1. 🎙
    Hears the commandTamil or English, voice or text
  2. 🔎
    Finds Shiva and “Anne”Lead by name and phone; property by name, code or reference
  3. 🗓
    Picks a free agentProperty agent first, then lead agent, then whoever is free at 2 pm
  4. ✅
    One-tap confirmPreview of booking and message
  5. 💬
    Confirms to ShivaWhatsApp: time, map pin, agent's name and number
  6. ⏰
    Reminds and assignsAgent task + reminder 2 h before; client reminder that morning

Flagship 2 · standard at Follow Up Boss, CINC, Sell.Do

Every call updates the lead by itself

Calls go through a business number, are recorded with consent and transcribed in Tamil and English. The lead AI that already reads chats reads the call too.

TODAYAfter every call

  1. 1Open the lead
  2. 2Type what was said
  3. 3Decide the next follow-up
  4. 4Update interest and stage
  5. 5Book the visit if agreed

5 steps × every call, every agent, every day — and skipped when busy

WITH AIHang up; it is done

  1. 📞
    Call recorded with consentExotel / Ozonetel number mapped to the agent
  2. 📝
    TranscribedTamil + English speech-to-text
  3. 🧠
    Read by the lead AISummary, objections, properties discussed
  4. 📅
    Next follow-up setInside working hours; interest hot / warm / cold
  5. 🏠
    Visit and stage updatedForward only, undo for 7 days
  6. 👀
    Agent glances, keeps or edits“📞 Call summary” in the Timeline

After a visit, a 30-second voice note becomes feedback, a follow-up and a second-visit proposal.

Flagship 3 · no vendor does this well — 3 PIN can lead

Seller autopilot: from “I want to sell” to matched buyers

The system collects, verifies, asks for approval, books the shoot, chases what is missing, lists the property and messages the buyers who fit. People step in only for decisions.

TODAYThe agent does it all

  2. 2Ask for papers, follow up for days

  4. 4Find a shoot slot that suits everyone
  5. 5Request the brochure
  6. 6Post on 99acres and the website by hand
  7. 7Work out which buyers to call

7 hand-offs; cards wait in “Details & docs”

    WhatsApp intakeAsks only for what is missing against the 30-field checklist
  2. 🔍
    Documents read and checkedPatta reference / QR, EC, owner ID; mismatches flagged
  3. 👍

  4. ✍️

It also adds the two owner gates the app lacks today: approval and agreement.

The next layer · 27 advanced ideas

1 Nightly fact storeMonthsHigh impactCompresses leads, milestones, signals, edits, posts and the ledger nightly, so every analysis costs about 30 reads.
2 3 PIN MCP serverMonthsHigh impactAsk Claude or ChatGPT about live data: “which Anna Nagar buyers above ₹2 Cr went quiet after a visit?”
3 Supervised AI back officeQuartersHigh impactFive agents (inside sales, listing, transaction, media, finance clerk) on one queue; a person approves each action.
4 Self-audit and ops sentinelWeeksMedium impactMeasures AI accuracy weekly; watches syncs, queues and the Mac pipeline and repairs the safe cases.

BSales intelligence

5 Deal score and next actionMonthsHigh impactA transparent score with a one-line why; advisory only, never moves a stage.
6 Going-cold warningWeeksMedium impactFlags a lead before it goes silent and drafts one nudge for approval.
7 Parked-buyer revivalWeeksHigh impactNew stock or a price drop matches a lost or held buyer, with a ready message.
8 Pre-visit negotiation briefWeeksHigh impactMust-haves, objections with quotes, the seller's floor, comparables, three questions to ask.
9 Objection libraryWeeksMedium impact“Anne: parking raised in 4 of 6 visits”, and the replies that worked.
10 “Likely to move” engineMonthsHigh impactLease ends, rent escalations, past buyers' life events: signals 3 PIN owns.
11 Coaching flywheelMonthsMedium impactCalls and chats scored against 3 PIN's own playbook; Tamil role-play.

14 Data-quality agentMonthsMedium impactProposes missing fields from details and chats; drafts the exact sheet edits.
15 “3 PIN Verified” badgeMonthsHigh impactOwner KYC, patta QR, 30-year EC, RERA and forgery checks: trust as a brand.

ECommerce, finance and new revenue

21 WhatsApp-native commerceMonthsHigh impactFlows for requirements and slots, listing carousels, fee payments, ads optimised for visits.
