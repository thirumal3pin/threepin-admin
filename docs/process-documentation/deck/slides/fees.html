Chennai norms in 2026: resale 1–2 % a side, rentals one month's rent, channel partners 1–2 %, sole-selling mandates 2–4 %, NoBroker management 8–10 % (5–8 % on offer). Sell-in-30 qualifies only if the ask sits inside the price-note range, the papers pass Verified and the seller allows visits on at least five days in the first two weeks; "under offer" means a token paid. Both fees are earned only when the flat sells, so the real risk is reputation — hence strict conditions and published results. Unit economics are on today's placeholders: nearly half of each fee goes on winning the deal because 49 of 50 leads do not convert, which is why conversion is worth more than any new channel and Wave 1 comes first. A listing pays for itself above about ₹35 L at 1 %; below that, offer the Normal listing without the full kit. Keeping a client costs about ₹500 a year. The fee card lives in Settings → Configurations; no fee below the card without the owner's approval.
