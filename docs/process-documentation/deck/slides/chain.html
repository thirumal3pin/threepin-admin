5

Redevelopment

2028 · risk medium, long cycle

  • Old apartments: society outreach, owner consensus, developer selection
  • Facilitation, surplus-unit mandate, management

Every unit enters the passport, management and resale cycle

Illustrative numbers are assumptions to test on the first project. No construction before two JVs have closed; a separate legal entity and accounts; capital capped per project; escrow for buyer money; RERA where thresholds apply.

The case in one line: a brokerage knows what sells; a developer builds what it hopes sells. Chennai JVs give landowners 40–55 % of built-up area in established areas; projects over 500 sq m or eight units register with TNRERA; sole-selling mandates pay 2–4 % against 1–2 % for ordinary channel partners. One boutique project a year plus three mandates on others' projects exceeds the brokerage line in the financial model. Twelve months: pick 3 mandate targets by December, first two mandates and the first report by March, first JV by June, second JV and the co-development site by September, term sheet and bookings in October 2027.
