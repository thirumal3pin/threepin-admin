The proof is public. SERHANT's S.MPLE 2.0 and its AI chief of staff, Dot: about 28,000 hours of admin returned and commission income up 144 % year on year for the agents who adopted it (company-reported). Compass agents report 15–20 hours a week back.

Revenue per sales agent: almost eight times in the base case, with two agents added. Every input is an assumption.

This is the turn from today to the proposal. Three levers, each measured in the CRM and Finance: conversion from 2 % to 5 % of leads, volume from 70 to 160 leads a month and from 8 to 20 listings, and value per deal up about 40 % as the ticket rises to ₹1.1 Cr and half the deals carry both sides. Together they take monthly brokerage from about ₹1.7 L to about ₹13 L, and total revenue with loans, services and management to about ₹17 L by October 2028. "Today" is a placeholder: if Finance shows a higher base, the multiples fall while the rupee gains stay. Already built at 3 PIN: the pipeline. Missing: the layer that acts on it, the channels that reach people at scale, the recurring lines.
