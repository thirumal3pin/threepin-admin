Proves it: outreach-to-listing ≥ 5 % by month 6

B · Onboarding & listing

Today: staff type the details; no price opinion, agreement workflow or enforced approval gate.

  • WhatsApp intake asks the 30 fields in Tamil or English
  • Document AI and a “3 PIN Verified” badge
  • Chennai price desk: a range with reasons, monthly
  • Instant listing presentation: PDF and 60-second video
  • E-signed agreement; the owner's one-tap approval gates the shoot

Money (assumption): exclusive share 20 % → 50 %; a second side ≈ ₹1 L per ₹1 Cr deal.

Proves it: owner-yes → live ≤ 7 days; exclusive share

Day after, sourcing: each agent's Daily task gains a Sourcing section with five owners to contact, the script ready and the waiting buyers named; the Sellers view gets a Prospect column.
