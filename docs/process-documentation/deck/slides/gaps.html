Gaps against the owner's process

Four decisions the app does not hold yet

Swami's approval

Not recorded anywhere. "Owner approved" on the card means the property owner approved the photos.

Agreement gate

Exclusive and Collab should be signed before the shoot; listing type is recorded but gates nothing.

Document alerts

The checklist never blocks, as wanted — but it never reminds anyone either.

Post plan first

Editing can start before Swami has planned the post and the voice-over.

Where the app is better than the reference: per-outlet edits and V/O, posts confirmed by a person with the URL, mapping by hand, a card for every seller automatically.

All four gaps are closed by Flagship 3, the seller autopilot, which adds one-tap approval cards and e-signed agreements. The documents lane stays non-blocking but gets nudges.
