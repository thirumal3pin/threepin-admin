Recurring revenue, a chief of staff, the moat

I · After-sale & maintain

Today: none — a sold property disappears; a rented one is not managed.

  • Rental and property management at 8–10 % of rent, with an NRI tier
  • Services marketplace: loans, interiors, movers, legal, insurance
  • Annual home value report to every past client
  • Client club and referrals; investor products via regulated partners

Money (assumption): 100 units × ₹25k × 9 % ≈ ₹2.7 L a month.

Proves it: recurring share ≥ 25 %

J · The agent back office

Today: Daily task and digests; the agent still types everything.

  • Eåsy, proactive: drafts, briefs and approvals ready before 9 am
  • Voice and text commands in Tamil or English
  • Generators: presentation, price note, visit pack, offer letter
  • A weekly report that writes itself; one approval queue, 7-day undo

Money (assumption): 10 h a week each ≈ 2–3 extra deals a month.

Proves it: hours returned, measured

K · Data & intelligence

Today: rich event data is stored and read by almost nothing.

  • Nightly fact store, topic by topic, inside the free quota
  • Chennai price desk and demand map, monthly
  • Attribution: lead → source → post → visit → deal → ₹
  • AI self-audit and ops sentinel; the 3 PIN MCP server

Money: the base for all of it; lead views already cut reads 760 → 39.

Proves it: answers without an engineer

NoBroker reached ₹1,000 crore on exactly these recurring lines; brokerage income is lumpy, management income is monthly, and the relationship after the sale is where the next listing comes from. Management needs an operations person, not agents — start with 20 units and SLAs. The back office is the measured core of the SERHANT and Compass results: 10 hours a week per agent is about 250 productive hours a month for six people. A small team with a chief of staff behaves like a team twice the size. The data — milestone dates, undos, chat signals, price edits, edit timings, post live times — is the one thing competitors cannot buy; the fact store keeps reads inside the free quota.
