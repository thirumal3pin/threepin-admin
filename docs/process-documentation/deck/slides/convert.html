H · Transaction & closing

Today: Finance holds the deal; the road to registration lives in chats.

  • Coordinator: token → agreement → KYC → loan → registration → handover
  • E-stamp, e-sign, KYC and PMLA records
  • Registration intelligence: guideline value, stamp duty, slots
  • Post-registration follow-through starts the after-sale

Money (assumption): fall-through after token 10 % → 4 %.

Proves it: token → registration ≤ 45 days

Response time is the largest conversion lever: a lead answered within 5 minutes converts 8–9 times better; Indian voice agents report three times the qualification rate. The phone is still where Chennai closes. The model assumes about 70 leads a month, so 2 % → 5 % is two more deals a month. Day after: no missed call after hours, every call has a note in two minutes, half of first "visits" happen on the phone, every open deal shows its stage and next action. Visits per deal 12 → 7 is +40 % agent capacity; loan income runs ₹20–80k on an ₹80 L loan and the model counts ₹40k net after any DSA share. Agent time: −5 hours of notes and chasing, −4 hours of unproductive visits, −3 hours per active deal. Guardrails: recording consent and DPDP from May 2027, TRAI (no cold AI calls), legal advice stays with advocates, cost sheets checked monthly.
