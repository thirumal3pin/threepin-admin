Admins see everything and decide who sees what. Agents see their own properties and leads, and a summary of the rest. Full access staff see everything and can set a property’s agents, but can’t change roles or what agents see.

Agents ask for a property’s details (or a matching buyer’s number) from their Properties page. The property’s primary agent or any admin decides — the first decision wins. Everything shared is listed here, and you can take it back at any time.

Saved changes reach agents straight away: the menu, what they see of each property and lead, and what they may do.
